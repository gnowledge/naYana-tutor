FONTLOG for Nayana
==================

Nayana is a derivative of Comic Neue (Craig Rozynski, 2014) modified to
add OpenType features that progressively transform English text toward
phonetic spelling. The base font glyphs are unmodified; phase-specific
glyph variants and GSUB lookups are layered on as stylistic sets (ss01,
ss02, ...).

This font is part of the naYana project of the gnowledge lab at the
Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education, TIFR Mumbai. naYana proposes
a phased phonetic reform of English orthography. Each phase adds one
visual change. All phases ship in the same font; users opt into each
via OpenType feature toggles.
